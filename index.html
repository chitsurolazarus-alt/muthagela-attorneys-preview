About the Firm

A modern law firm with a deep commitment to community.

Founded and led by Director Ndivhuwo Charlotte Muthagela, Muthagela Attorneys Inc was built on a simple belief: quality legal representation should be within reach of everyone, not just a few.

From our offices on Christiaan De Wet Street, our team of attorneys and notaries assists clients with everything from drafting a will or an ante-nuptial contract to complex commercial, municipal and mining matters.
